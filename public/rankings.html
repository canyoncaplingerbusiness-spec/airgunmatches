How the ranking works. Your standing is the number of targets you have actually hit over the last twelve months. Every competitor appears from their first result. Turning out builds your total, but only while you are hitting — 500 targets at 92% is 460 hits and beats 1,000 targets at 40%, which is 400. Each discipline is ranked separately, because a field target card and a bench rest group were never the same currency. Disciplines with no fixed number of targets, such as group size, are ranked on finishing position instead. Browse upcoming matches →
