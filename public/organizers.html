The two kinds of code

This trips people up, so it's worth ninety seconds. There are two, they do very different jobs, and only one of them is yours to keep.

Your event code
ABCD-EFGH-JKMN

Keep this one to yourself. It's emailed to you when your event is approved. It opens the director console and the results upload — everything on this page. Anyone holding it can change your match, so treat it like a password.

A squad code
7K4M-PD9T-2XRB

These are for handing out. You generate one per squad in the director console. A squad code reaches that squad's competitors and nobody else's — a scorer holding it can't see or change another squad's scores, and can't touch your match settings.

Lost a phone, or a code got passed around further than you meant? Open the director console and press New code on that squad. The old one stops working immediately and every other squad carries on unaffected — nobody else has to re-enter anything.

How a scored match runs

  1. Before the day. Open the director console with your event code. For each discipline, choose how it's scored — stage by stage for field target, precision rifle and silhouette, or direct entry for bench rest where you grade the target and key in the score.
  2. Set out the match. Add your stages and how many shots each one has. Stages can be different lengths. Then add your competitors and split them into squads.
  3. Hand out the codes. Each squad gets its own. Copy the scorer link and text it to whoever is scoring that squad — it opens the scoring page with the code already filled in.
  4. Open the match. Scorers can now enter scores. Nothing can be scored before you open it, and nothing after you close it.
  5. Watch it come in. The console shows how much of the match is recorded and which squads are behind. You can show live standings publicly, or keep them to yourself until the end.
  6. Declare and publish. If two competitors tie, the system leaves it as a tie — it won't break one for you. Tick the winner and everyone below moves down. Then publish, and the results go on your event's page and into the national rankings.
No signal at your range? That's the normal case, and the scoring page is built for it. Each scorer opens the page once while they have a bar of signal — after that every tap is saved on their phone and sent automatically whenever the connection comes back. They can close the browser, lose signal all day, and nothing is lost.

Questions we get asked

What does it cost? Nothing. Listing an event, scoring it and posting results are all free.
Do I need an account? No. Your event code is the only thing that unlocks your match.
I've lost my event code. Get in touch and we'll reissue it to the email address on your submission.
Can two people score the same squad? Yes. If they both edit the same competitor on the same stage, the second one is shown both versions side by side with who entered each, and picks. Nothing is ever silently overwritten.
My match has several disciplines. Each one is set up and scored separately, and can use a different scoring method. Publish them as they finish.
Can I fix a score after publishing? Yes. Reopen the discipline, correct it, and publish again. The rankings update with it.
What happens to competitors' details? Only names, placings and scores are ever public. Your contact details as the submitter are never shown on the site.
