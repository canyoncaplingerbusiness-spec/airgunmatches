# Club outreach emails

Send from **Canyoncaplinger.business@gmail.com**. Plain text. No logo, no HTML,
no attachment on first contact — those get filtered, and a club secretary reading
on a phone wants four sentences, not a brochure.

Replace everything in `[brackets]`. If you can't fill in the specific detail,
the email isn't ready to send — a generic one reads as a mailshot and gets deleted.

---

## 1. To a club whose match you've already listed

**Your best opener, and you have about 25 of these.** They're not being asked for
anything. Something good has already happened to them.

> **Subject:** Your [month] match is listed on GunMatches.com
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I've been building a national calendar of shooting matches, and your
> [match name] on [date] is on it — I found it on [where you found it], so I
> wanted to let you know rather than have you stumble across it:
>
> [direct link to the event]
>
> Have a look and tell me if anything's wrong. I'd rather fix a date or a fee than
> have a shooter drive to the wrong place.
>
> Two things you can do with it, both free and neither requiring an account:
>
> Post your results afterwards — paste them straight out of a spreadsheet and
> they appear on your event's page, so competitors can find their placing later.
> They also feed a national ranking, which is something airgun competition has
> never really had.
>
> Or score the match live from a phone. Your squad scorers tap hits and misses,
> standings build as the match runs, and it keeps working with no signal — which
> I'm told describes most ranges.
>
> There's no charge for any of it and there never will be. I'm not selling
> anything.
>
> Canyon Caplinger
> gunmatches.com

## 2. Cold, to a club you haven't listed

Use once there are a few firearm events on the calendar. Before that, use it for
airgun clubs only.

> **Subject:** Free listing for [club name]'s matches
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I run gunmatches.com — a national calendar of shooting matches, filterable by
> state and by what it's shot with. Airgun, rimfire, centerfire, shotgun, all on
> one page.
>
> I'd like to list [club name]'s [discipline] matches. It's free, takes about two
> minutes, and I review every submission by hand so nothing junk gets on there:
>
> gunmatches.com/organizers.html
>
> To be straightforward with you: the site is new and most of what's on it today
> is airgun competition. I'm working outward from there. If you list your matches
> you'll be early, and you'll be in front of shooters who don't already follow
> your club.
>
> It won't replace how you run your matches. If you use PractiScore for
> registration, keep using it — just link to it from your listing. This is about
> being findable by people who don't know to look for you yet.
>
> Canyon Caplinger
> gunmatches.com

## 3. Follow-up

**Once. Ten to fourteen days later.** If they don't reply to this, leave them be
and try again next season — a nagged club secretary tells the other club
secretaries.

> **Subject:** Re: [original subject]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Just closing the loop on this. If listing your matches isn't useful, no problem
> at all and I won't chase it again.
>
> If it's just that you haven't had a minute: send me the dates and venue in a
> reply and I'll put them up myself. Easier than filling in a form.
>
> Canyon

## 4. To an association or governing body

Slower, but one yes is worth fifty club emails. AAFTA, state associations,
regional leagues, series organisers.

> **Subject:** Listing [association]'s match schedule — free, no account needed
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I've built gunmatches.com, a free national calendar of shooting matches. It
> currently carries [number] events across [number] states, most of them airgun
> competition, and I'm expanding into rimfire, centerfire and shotgun.
>
> I'd like to get [association]'s sanctioned matches listed. Two things that might
> matter to you:
>
> Every listing is reviewed by hand before it goes live, so your name won't end up
> next to something that isn't a real match. And posted results build national
> rankings by finishing place, so a shooter's season is visible across every club
> they compete at rather than one at a time.
>
> It's free, there are no ads, and there's no account for shooters to create.
>
> If it's useful, I can list your published schedule myself — send me a link and
> I'll do the typing. If you'd rather each club submitted their own, that works too.
>
> Happy to answer anything, or to take it down if you'd prefer I didn't list them.
>
> Canyon Caplinger
> gunmatches.com

## Answers to what they'll ask

**"What's the catch? What does it cost?"**
Nothing, and there is no plan for it to cost anything. It runs on free
infrastructure — genuinely about a dollar a month in domain fees. No ads, and no
selling anyone's details.

**"Do I need an account?"**
No. Neither do your shooters. Approved events give you a code by email — that's
what unlocks results upload and live scoring.

**"We already use PractiScore."**
Keep using it. Put the PractiScore link in your listing. This is a calendar, not
a registration system.

**"Who sees my email address?"**
Nobody. It's used to send your event code and to check details with you. It's
never shown on the site.

**"Can we take it down later?"**
Yes. Email and it's gone the same day.

**"How many people actually use it?"**
Be honest: it's new. Say the number of events, say the site launched this month,
and say you'd rather it grew properly than looked bigger than it is. Clubs
respect that far more than a bluff — and in a community this small, a bluff gets
found out by the weekend.
